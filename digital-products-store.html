Family Tree Error Check

Online trees copy each other’s mistakes until a guess looks like gospel. Send us the tree you already have, and we’ll audit it line by line — you get back a downloadable written report of everything that doesn’t hold up, and how we know, plus your tree file so you can use the results in your own software.

What we check for

  • Impossible dates — children born before their parents, mothers aged 8 or 68, people marrying after their own funerals
  • Unsourced claims stated as fact — names, dates, and places with no record behind them
  • Likely duplicates — the same person entered twice under spelling variants
  • Conflated identities — two same-named people merged into one impossible life
  • Conflicting records — censuses, certificates, and memorials that disagree with each other

How it works — automatically

  1. Buy only this check & send your tree One-time payment, no subscription. Send a GEDCOM file or an export from your tree software or site — whatever you have.
  2. The audit runs automatically Every person and claim checked against the dates, the relationships, and the records.
  3. Download your file A downloadable written error report — each finding labeled, with the sources that were checked — plus your tree file, so you can fix your tree with confidence and keep using it anywhere else.

Every finding carries a label — the same standard we use in our own research. No finding is dressed up as more certain than it is:

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Genealogy Detective Service (“we,” “us”) provides genealogy research services — family tree error checks, research tiers, and single research services — delivered digitally by file and email to customers worldwide. Questions about this policy or your information: admin@tree-detective.com.

What we collect

We collect only what an order or a free membership actually needs:

  • Contact details — your name and email address, so we can confirm your order, ask about scope, and deliver your files.
  • Research material you send us — the names, dates, and places you provide, and any family tree file you upload (such as a GEDCOM, up to 8 MB) with a research request.
  • Order details — which service or tier you bought, the scope agreed, and the price quoted.
  • Membership details — if you join the free membership, your name and email, so we can send the weekly newsletter and honor the member discount.

We never see or store your card number. Payments are handled by our payment provider; card details go straight to them and never pass through us. We see only your name, email, and what you bought.

How we use it

  • To carry out the research you ordered and assemble your report and tree file.
  • To contact you about your order — a scope question, a quote, or delivery.
  • If you are a member, to send the weekly newsletter with members-only coupons. Every issue can be the last one: ask us to stop and we stop.
  • To keep basic business records of orders and payments.

That is the whole list. We do not sell your information, rent it, or share it for anyone else’s marketing. Your family details and tree files are never published, posted, or added to any public tree.

How your information is stored

  • Names, emails, memberships, and research requests are kept in the member app’s private database. Only the owner of Genealogy Detective Service can see the full member and request lists.
  • Passwords are never stored as readable text — they are kept as a salted, scrambled hash, so the database itself does not contain anyone’s actual password.
  • Uploaded tree files are held in private file storage, separate from the request itself. A request holds only a reference and the file name, and downloading a customer’s tree file requires the owner’s login.
  • This public webpage stores nothing at all — it has no working forms. Sign-ups and orders happen in the member app.

Family trees often include living relatives. Please send only what the research needs, and only information you have the right to share. We treat everything you send — about living people and ancestors alike — as confidential research material: used for your order, never published, and never passed to another customer.

How long we keep it

We keep order records and delivered research for as long as they are needed for the business records of the sale and in case you return with a follow-up question about your report. Tree files you upload are kept with their request so the work can be checked against them. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time — see below.
